# Constitution: engineering-manager 1.0.0
Compiled from the organisation’s Confluence pages and approved by a process owner. Rules marked enforced are checked by hooks or CI; advisory rules are guidance.
## Review rubric

### PRDs define success metrics (`pm.prd.metrics`)

Every PRD states measurable success metrics with targets.

Source: page 300001 v1 — "State how you will measure success"

### PRDs list non-goals (`pm.prd.non-goals`)

Every PRD lists what is out of scope.

Source: page 300001 v1 — "List what you are not doing"

### Designs explain rollback (`eng.design.rollback`)

design.md says how to roll the change back.

Source: page 100002 v1 — "Describe how you would roll back"

### PRDs state latency targets (`prd.nfr.latency`)

User-facing lookups state a p95 latency target per region.

Source: page 300001 v1 — "State latency targets for anything a user waits on"

### PRDs plan rollout and rollback (`prd.rollout.plan`)

Every PRD orders its rollout and says how to roll back.

Source: page 300001 v1 — "Say how you will roll out and how you would roll back"

## Advisory

### PRD reviews within 5 working days (`prd.review.sla`)

Reviewers respond to a PRD within 5 working days.

Source: page 300001 v1 — "Reviewers respond within five working days"

### 1:1 briefs are preparation aids (`em.oneonone.not-evaluation`)

Use 1:1 briefs to prepare conversations, never as performance evidence or for pay and promotion decisions.

Source: page 400001 v1 — "Briefs prepare a conversation; they are never performance evidence"

### Reports see the same evidence (`em.oneonone.shared-evidence`)

Share brief sources with the report and invite their context before the 1:1.

Source: page 400001 v1 — "the report sees the same evidence"

### Design approvals need a staff engineer (`em.review.design-approval`)

Managers do not approve high-tier designs on behalf of a staff engineer.

Source: page 100002 v1 — "High-risk designs are approved by a staff engineer"
